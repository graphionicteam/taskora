# Taskora — Brand Guidelines
**Status:** Core identity choices approved and locked as UI/UX Stage 04 (2026-10-12).

## Brand positioning
Taskora is a premium, collaborative productivity SaaS. The identity should feel trustworthy, calm, polished, easy to use and enduring. 70% practical clarity / 30% premium expression.

## Wordmark and mark
- Official displayed name **Taskora** (title case).
- Approved concept: **A1 Interlocking Layers**.
- Flat, solid, gently rounded connected layer geometry; **no gradients, glass, shadows-as-logo, or 3D effects**.
- Main mark color **Refined Indigo `#4F46E5`**.
- Wordmark uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** with confident, legible spacing.
- Logo should remain clear in header, collapsed sidebar, authentication screens and future app icon.
- Original approved concept/artwork is reference; do not claim the existing PNG is a final editable SVG master. Confirm vector asset provenance/export before shipping.

## Color roles
- Primary action, selected navigation, interactive emphasis: Refined Indigo `#4F46E5`.
- HIMI AI only: Electric Violet `#8B5CF6` (use accessible text variants for actual content).
- Light neutrals for backgrounds/surfaces and readable ink/dark neutrals for typography.
- Status and priority semantic colors come from the locked implementation palette; do not invent extra brand colors.

## Typography and imagery
- Plus Jakarta Sans 400/500/600/700 across brand/UI.
- Strong readability and whitespace; restrained hierarchy, clean icons, no gratuitous illustration-heavy visuals.
- Lucide-consistent line icon language; visual consistency across web and future Flutter.

## Do / Don't
**Do:** flat logo, purposeful spacing, readable UI, understated depth, carefully controlled hover/active states.
**Don't:** gradients on logo, random violet accents everywhere, generic glowing AI aesthetic, decorative clutter, rebrand without approval, merge HIMI identity into Taskora logo.
